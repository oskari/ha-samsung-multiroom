Make a group

The main speaker plays the music; the others play along in sync. A Spotify bridge on the main speaker plays to the whole group.

Add a Spotify Connect speaker

Creates a Spotify Connect device that plays on the chosen speaker (or on a whole group, if you pick its main speaker).

Connect a new speaker to Wi-Fi

  1. Plug the speaker in and switch it on.
  2. Hold the WPS/Wi-Fi SETUP button on its back for about 5 seconds, until its light changes. It now offers a setup Wi-Fi called "[Samsung] ...".
  3. Below, find that Wi-Fi and tell the speaker which home network to join.

Other ways

  • WPS: press your router's WPS button, then the speaker's WPS/Wi-Fi SETUP button for about 1 second.
  • Ethernet: plug the speaker into your router; it shows up under Speakers, where Change Wi-Fi moves it onto Wi-Fi.
